# Digital Transformation Strategy for Prometeon

## Overview

This repository presents a digital transformation case study developed for Prometeon. The project examines the company's existing business processes, identifies operational challenges, and proposes practical digital solutions aimed at improving efficiency, customer experience, and long-term business performance.

The study follows a structured business analysis approach, covering the current state of operations, business needs, solution recommendations, implementation planning, and expected business outcomes.

---

## Project Objectives

The objective of this project is to evaluate existing business processes and develop a practical digital transformation strategy that supports sustainable growth and operational improvement.

The proposed solutions focus on:

- Improving operational efficiency
- Reducing manual processes
- Enhancing customer interaction
- Supporting data-driven decision making
- Building a scalable digital infrastructure

---

## Business Challenges

The analysis identified several key challenges within the current business environment:

- Lack of an online sales platform
- Manual financial data management using Excel
- No live customer support solution
- Limited process automation
- Inefficient data organization and reporting

---

## Proposed Solutions

The digital transformation strategy includes the following initiatives:

- E-commerce integration
- Financial workflow automation with Airtable
- Live chat implementation
- Business process automation
- KPI-based performance monitoring
- Cybersecurity improvements
